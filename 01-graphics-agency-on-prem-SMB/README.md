## Project description

DreamMaker Studio is a creative agency with 1 CEO + 15 employees located in Northern Italy specializing in digital graphics, video production, and 3D rendering. Over the past year, 4K video production has become more frequent, and they've also landed clients requiring static 3D modeling and, occasionally, the inclusion of 3D models in videos.

Last year, they budgeted to overhaul their entire IT infrastructure for two reasons: 
1. their workstations are dated.
2. their data management is not quite organized with minimal manual backup

What prompted them to take action was a hard drive failure that resulted in the loss of several weeks of work when they were managing a large client that had requested the production of several 4K videos and 3D models.

Thus, they want to implement a resilient structure keeping internal storage with limited external access from the CEO and the sales people. 

The working force is as follows:
* Company owner/CEO. He's a VFX artist but doesn't work as one. He has a laptop because he often works from anywhere. He's responsible for maintaining relationships with major clients, the company's vision, and its expansion. He wants a secure way to access his data remotely. He holds a lot of webcam conferences.
* 2 salespeople. The two salespeople have a personal tablet that they use only for work purposes. They manage schedules, appointments, and take notes on negotiations. They rarely appear in the office and mainly interact with the project manager and the administrative staff as they bring contracts/take them and confirm the feasibility (and correct margin).
* 1 Project manager: uses a laptop. After the order is placed, he or she goes to the clients to manage the project, provide presentations, and handle specific technical matters. He or she uses a laptop but has a monitor and a workstation in the office.
* 1 person handles administration. Specifically, documentation, and customer relations via email and telephone. He or she has a fixed workstation in the office.
* 1 person handles secretarial/front office work. Fixed desktop workstation.
* 2 people in the graphics department which handle 2D graphics. Photoshop and Illustrator are the most commonly used programs. They have fixed desktop workstations.
* 2 people in the video production department. They are the ones who actually shoot the footage and have a fixed desktop workstation where they upload the files and need to be able to save them somewhere to share with the editors.
* 2 people handle editing, cutting, post-production, color grading, color correction, and rendering. These people also handle audio. Dedicated desktop workstation. Premiere Pro is the most used software. 
* 1 motion designer/compositor. Integrates the 3D model and creates VFX. Dedicated desktop workstation. Uses After Effects practically exclusively. Dedicated workstation.
* 1 person handles 3D modeling. Dedicated workstation. Mainly uses Blender and 3ds Max. 
* 1 person handles data wrangling and video script management.

The allocated budget for everything, including margin, is expected to be €69,500 in the first year. 
Software licenses of any kind are not included.
